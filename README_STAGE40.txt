AIR TRAVEL STAGE 40 – BOOKING REQUEST CENTRE

Members must sign in to submit a flight booking request. Requests are saved privately to the member account and can be reviewed by the owner at /owner/booking-requests. The owner can mark requests Pending, Contacted or Closed.

IMPORTANT: This is a working enquiry/request workflow, not live airline ticketing. No live fares, seat inventory, airline reservation, ticket issuance, or payment processing is connected. Before real bookings are offered, an authorised adult administrator must integrate a legitimate airline or booking provider and arrange secure payment and customer-support processes. Do not collect passport or payment-card details in this demo.
